CRO Page Prioritization

Where the Conversion
Leaks Are

The single most valuable conversion mechanism on this site is the homepage hero's ability to guide visitors to a clear, primary action. Currently, the hero presents multiple competing CTAs ('Shop All', 'Shop Womens', 'Shop Mens', etc.) without a single primary focus, diluting user attention and increasing cognitive load. This is bleeding value by preventing visitors from quickly finding the most relevant product category, thereby reducing the likelihood of immediate engagement and purchase.
